About Upright Publications

Who We Are

Upright Publications is an international scholarly publishing platform committed to advancing high-quality research and promoting the global exchange of knowledge. We publish peer-reviewed academic journals across diverse disciplines with a strong emphasis on research integrity, editorial excellence, and open scholarly communication.

Our Mission

Our mission is to support researchers by providing a trusted, transparent, and efficient publishing environment that promotes scientific innovation, academic collaboration, and responsible research dissemination.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a globally recognized academic publisher that connects researchers, institutions, and professionals through high-quality scholarly publications that create meaningful impact.

Our Core Values

  • Academic Integrity
  • Editorial Independence
  • Rigorous Peer Review
  • Research Excellence
  • Transparency and Ethics
  • Global Collaboration
